# VillaNET 3.3.0: build contract

Written 2026-10-13 for the second item of Victor's request of that day, the one 3.2.1 left for
previews (`design/3.2.1.md`). In his words:

> 2. Have the clip viewer player be the same aesthetic as the main video stream player.

It is a visible redesign, so he saw previews first: three looks built as real code in a sandbox,
shot in the lab at 400 px, 304 px and in a 1500 × 1000 window, beside 3.2.1's player and the stream
tile. The preview's code and montages are kept in `Broadcast Box Plugin.patches/previews-3.3.0/`
(`src/`, `lab/`, `webapp.diff`, `montage/viewer-*.png`).

> **Victor's picks, 2026-10-13 (binding).**
>
> | Asked | Victor's pick | What it means |
> |---|---|---|
> | Which look? | **A, "Tile".** | The stream tile's frame and its line under the picture. The controls float over the bottom of the picture while it is pointed at or has focus: play and pause, the sound and its volume, a red scrubber, Download, full screen. At rest there is only the picture, the name and the time. |
> | Where does Download go? | **In the player.** | A download icon among the player's controls, through 3.2.1's own ticket (`downloadClip`, `saveClipFrom`), with its words on the tile's status line. 3.2.1's DOWNLOAD pill after Share goes, so there is only ever one Download, and only while `canDownload`. |
> | What does the name line say? | **The streamer only.** | No game or movie chip and no stream title on it: the clip's title is the heading just under the player. |
>
> Looks B ("Tile and control row") and C ("Switch") were not chosen and are not built.

**Victor's standing rules**, unchanged and binding here as everywhere:

- The panel is locked until a person unlocks it with their own bearer token ("linked"). Admins can let
  people in to watch only. Interactive features are linked-only, and the server enforces it.
- Downloads are as 3.2.1 made them: off until an admin allows them, linked people only, a streamer can
  say no, never on shared cards or in the System Console.
- Browsers contact only the Mattermost server, apart from Twitch's player and Connect.
- Previews before any visible redesign: this is the build of the previewed look A, and nothing else
  changes how anything looks.
- Only Mattermost 11.11 is supported (React 18.2.0).

**Precedence:** Victor's picks, then this document, then `3.2.1.md` (downloads), then `clips-spec.md`
and `clips-addendum.md`.

## 1. The player (look A)

### 1.1 What it shows

- **The frame:** `.vntv-player` (the tile's 2 px outline, black), the tile's 16:9 `Screen`, and the
  tile's line under it (`TileInfo`): the streamer's face and name, and at the end of the line the
  time, "0:12 / 0:45", in the display face, the length in the quieter grey.
- **Pointed at, or with focus inside it:** the stream tile's shade along the bottom with white icons,
  red on hover: Play/Pause/Replay, Mute/Unmute, the volume, then (right) Download when offered and
  Full screen. Under them, the scrubber with its handle.
- **Paused:** all of that stays up with nobody pointing at it (`keepUp`).
- **Playing, at rest:** only the picture, the name, the time, and a slim red line along the picture's
  bottom edge (3 px, on a dark track so it reads over a bright picture), which lifts into the scrubber
  when the controls come up.
- **Red is where the clip is:** the scrubber's line and handle. The volume's handle, red on a stream
  (which has no timeline), is white in a clip.
- **Loading** (the file not there within 300 ms): the tile's tuning burst with the plate
  "LOADING" over the clip's title; stalled while playing: a ring in the glass of the stream tile's
  switch. **Can't play:** static on black, the plate "CAN'T PLAY", and why, with **Try again**, on the
  tile's status line, as a stream's failure is. Download, when offered, stays on the picture there as
  the one control left, coming up as the controls do (pointed at or tabbed to): a file this browser
  cannot play is the one most worth downloading (review fix ux-1, `playerControls`). A clip has no
  switch, so neither plate fades while its controls are up, as a stream's plate does to make room
  for the switch (review fix ux-4). Over LOADING the scrubber is hidden and out of play: no Tab stop,
  no clicks (review fix ux-3).
- A browser that cannot play the clip at all, or not its sound, gets 3.2.1's note under the tile.
- While the viewer fetches a clip it does not have yet, the player's empty frame stands in its place,
  the same size, so the dialog does not change shape when the clip arrives.

### 1.2 What it does

The rules are pure functions in `webapp/src/clipLook.ts`, checked by `build/check-player.mjs`:

- **Length:** the server's `durationMs`, but no longer than the file past its lead-in
  (`clipLength`, `fileSecondsOf`).
- **Position:** `clipAt(currentTime, lead, length)`, so the lead-in is off the line and nothing past
  the end is on it. Seeks land inside the clip (`seekTarget`).
- **The end:** within 50 ms of the length (`atEnd`, `END_SLACK`), and a playing clip is stopped when
  it reaches its own end (`ranPastEnd`), which comes before the file's when the file runs on.
- **The clock:** where it is, rounded down as a player's clock is, over the length; at the end it
  reads the length, so a 44.6 s clip ends at "0:45 / 0:45", never "0:44 / 0:45" (`clipClock`). The
  scrubber's `aria-valuetext` says the same ("0:12 of 0:45"); the clock itself is `aria-hidden`.
  While the scrubber has focus and the clip plays on, its value holds where it was when focus came
  or the reader last moved it, so a screen reader does not read the time every second; paused,
  dragged or moved by its keys it says where the clip is, once (`heardAt`, review fix ux-2).
- **The scrubber:** a press anywhere on it goes there, a drag scrubs with the pointer captured
  (`secondsAt`), and the clock moves with it. A clip that was playing plays on when it is let go,
  unless it was let go at the end. `touch-action: none`, so a drag on a phone does not scroll the
  dialog.
- **A click on the picture** (mouse) plays or pauses. A tap only wakes the controls or puts them
  away, as on a stream. Only a press that began on the bare picture counts: a press on a control
  slid off onto the picture before it is let go is taken back, as it is on any button (review fix
  code-2).
- **The sound** is 3.2.1's: playing with sound takes it from the stream that had it, and pausing,
  going back or closing gives it back. A file that fails while it plays is paused by the player, so
  that gives it back too, and the controls say Play; Try again pauses before it reloads (review fix
  code-1: Firefox fires no `pause` after a dropped connection, and `load()` stops a playing element
  silently).
- **Full screen** is the whole tile, so the name line and the controls go with it.

### 1.3 The keys

From anywhere in the player (`playerKey`): **Space** or **K** plays and pauses (Replay at the end);
**←/→** five seconds back or on; **J/L** ten; **M** mutes; **F** full screen. Space on a button presses
that button; an arrow on a slider moves that slider; nothing with Ctrl, Command or Alt, or while an
input method is composing; Escape and Tab go on to the dialog. The scrubber's own keys
(`scrubberKey`): arrows five seconds, Page Up/Down ten, Home and End to the ends. The player is a
labelled group that says its keys, and is focusable (never a Tab stop) so a click leaves the keys
working.

### 1.4 No other way to download

No `controls` on the `<video>`, so the browser's own menu and download button are gone with it, and
`onContextMenu` is prevented on the picture, so there is no **Save video as**. As 3.2.1 said of
`controlsList="nodownload"`: this deters, it cannot prevent; whatever a browser can play, it can save.

### 1.5 The Hud, opt-in

`Hud` gains `edge` (the scrubber, along the picture's bottom edge, drawn while the row sleeps),
`keepUp` (`data-keep-up`, everything held up), `onSurface` (a mouse click on the bare picture), and
draws no switch when given no `onPower`. `TileInfo` gains `end`. `Tuning` takes the plate's word
(default "Tuning"). A stream's tile passes none of them: in the lab, its 41 elements have the same
geometry and the same computed styles in 3.2.1 and 3.3.0, at 304, 400 and 776 px, at rest and
pointed at.

## 2. Download in the player

- Offered while `clipDownloadOffered(clip, access)`, exactly 3.2.1's rule, whatever the player is
  doing, CAN'T PLAY included (§1.1); the console passes none.
- Pressing it is 3.2.1's flow, unchanged: a ticket, then the browser's own save
  (`downloadClip`, `saveClipFrom`). While the ticket is asked for, the icon pulses (as the scissors
  do while they cut), its name and tooltip are "Preparing…", and the tile's status line says
  "Preparing…". Then "Your download has started.", or a refusal in red in 3.2.1's words ("Downloads
  are off.", "The streamer has turned off downloads of their clips.", "This clip has gone…", "Slow
  down a little.", "The download could not be started just now.").
- It is `aria-disabled` while Report or Delete is busy, and pressing Report or Delete clears what it
  last said, as one line of words did for all three in 3.2.1.
- When a switch takes it away with focus on it, focus goes to the player, where the keys still work
  (`useFocusHome`). The row of pills keeps 3.2.1's rule for Report and Delete (`actionAfterLoss`).

## 3. The failure line is red

`.vntv-clipview-status.vntv-notice-error { color: var(--vntv-red-text); }`, after
`.vntv-clipview-status`, as `.vntv-hint.vntv-notice-error` already does for hints. The tile's own
status line takes the same lighter red (`.vntv-cliptile .vntv-player-status.vntv-error`).

## 4. The System Console

`AdminClips` plays a clip in its row in the same `ClipTile`, from the console's own routes, with
picture-in-picture off, and never with Download. On the console's white page the tile is a black
object with its dark red frame, as in the panel; **Try again**, in the error state, is the console's
own white pill, like the row's buttons just above it.

## 5. Tests

`build/check-player.mjs` (in `make check-webapp`): the length and the lead-in, seeks held to the clip,
the end and the stop at it, the play-through of "that jump" (lead 1.4 s), the scrubber's share and
what has loaded, a press and a drag past either end, the scrubber's keys, the clock (rounded down,
the length at the end, minutes), its words, the player's state and what it waits for, the keys (Space
on a button, K anywhere, arrows on a slider, J/L, M, F, modifiers, keys that are the dialog's), and
two guards on the source: Download is offered once, from the player, only under
`clipDownloadOffered`, and the console passes none; the `<video>` has no `controls` and blocks its
menu. `check-away.mjs` and the others are unchanged and green.

After the review (§1.1, §1.2): what the player's controls hold, Download kept on CAN'T PLAY
(`playerControls`, and that the tile draws by it); what a screen reader hears of the focused
scrubber (`heardAt`); and guards on the source for the scrubber out of play over LOADING, the
clip's plate not fading for its controls (with the stream's fade unchanged), and a press slid off a
control not counting as a click on the picture. 28 checks.
