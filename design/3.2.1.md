# VillaNET 3.2.1: build contract

Written 2026-10-13 for Victor's request of that day, sent while he was running 3.2.0 on his real
server. In his words:

> 1. Server toggle to allow/deny clip downloading.
> 2. Have the clip viewer player be the same aesthetic as the main video stream player.
> 3. Don't let users opt out of biggest fan.

**3.2.1 builds items 1 and 3.** Item 2 is a visible redesign, so Victor sees previews of it first; it is
being previewed separately and is not part of this release. Nothing here restyles the clip viewer.

> **Victor's answers, 2026-10-13 (binding).**
>
> | Item | Asked | Victor's answer | What it means |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 1 | Who may download? | **Linked people only.** | People let in to watch can watch clips but not download them. Locked people have no panel. |
> | 1 | Where is the button? | **In the clip viewer only.** | Not on the clip cards shared in chat. |
> | 1 | May a streamer say no? | **"Yes, a streamer switch."** | A streamer can block downloads of clips of their own stream, from **Clips of your stream**, even while the admin allows downloads. Allowed until they switch it off ("can block"). |
> | 1 | Default? | Downloads are **off** until an admin switches them on. | This changes 3.0.0's owner rule 6, "No download button" (`clips-addendum.md` A§1). |
> | 3 | What happens to the opt-out? | **It goes.** | Biggest fan is still counted only while **Show who is watching** is on (his 3.2.0 answer to Q1 stands). |

**Victor's standing rules**, unchanged and binding here as everywhere:

- The panel is locked until a person unlocks it with their own bearer token ("linked"). Admins can let
  people in to watch only. Interactive features are linked-only, and the server enforces it.
- Stream keys, tokens and secrets are never logged and never sent to browsers.
- Browsers contact only the Mattermost server, apart from Twitch's player and Connect. Downloads add no
  host: the file comes from the plugin's own route.
- The settings backup leaves secrets out unless ticked.
- Previews before any visible redesign. The two additions here (a Download pill beside Share, Report and
  Delete, and a second switch in a sheet that already has one) are built in the existing pieces' own
  style and are not a redesign.
- Only Mattermost 11.11 is supported; Mattermost behaviour is checked in its source.

**Precedence:** Victor's answers, then this document, then `3.2.0.md`, `clips-spec.md` and
`clips-addendum.md`.

## 0. Decisions in one screen

| # | Question | Decision | § |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The admin's switch | `AllowClipDownloads`, bool, **off**, in the **Clips** section after **Space for clips**: "Let people download clips". Not a secret, so the settings backup carries it (it is a configuration field, which is all the backup needs). | §1.1 |
| 2 | Who may download a clip | The server decides per reader and per clip, as `canDownload` on the clip in `GET /clips` and `GET /clips/clip`. All four must hold: the admin's switch is on; the reader is **linked** (`accessOf`); the reader may watch the clip by today's rules (it is still kept, and clips have somewhere to be kept); its streamer has not blocked downloads. | §1.2 |
| 3 | The streamer's switch | "Let people download clips of my stream" in **Clips of your stream** (`StreamerClips`), shown only while the admin allows downloads; on until switched off. Kept in the clips document beside the recording opt-out, by stream tag: `noDownloads`. It applies to every clip whose stream tag it is, past and future. | §1.3 |
| 4 | How a download is fetched | Never through `fetch` into memory: the browser saves it from the plugin's own address, through a temporary `<a href download>`. A **single-use ticket** (60 s, bound to the person and the clip) is asked for by a POST, which carries Mattermost's CSRF check; the GET that spends it hands the browser a **download address** for that one download. | §1.4 |
| 5 | Why two steps | Mattermost's desktop app cancels a download as it starts and asks for the same address again after its own save dialog; a browser resumes an interrupted download from the same address with a Range request. A ticket spent by the first request would make the second fail. So the ticket stays single-use and is spent once, and the address it hands out is the one the browser and the desktop app may ask again, for ten minutes, by that person only. This mirrors Connect Twitch, whose ticket is spent to make a state that lasts ten minutes. | §1.4 |
| 6 | The file | `Content-Disposition: attachment` with a clean name, `"<Streamer> - <title or 'clip'> - <YYYY-MM-DD HHMM>.mp4"`, in the reader's own Mattermost time zone; `video/mp4`, `nosniff`, `no-store`, a `sandbox` CSP, and Range support. Every download is logged with the user id and the clip id. | §1.5 |
| 7 | The biggest-fan opt-out | Removed end to end: the route, the document, the purge, the You tab's switch, the popover's takeback, the admin's restart, and their lab chips, types and checks. A leftover `fan_optouts` is deleted once, at activation, with a log line. | §2 |

## 1. Clip downloads

### 1.1 The setting

`plugin.json`, Clips section, after `ClipStorageLimitGB` and before `ClipsAdmin`:

- key `AllowClipDownloads`, type `bool`, default `false`, display name **Let people download clips**;
- help text: "Adds **Download** to a clip, for people who have linked their stream with their bearer
  token. People let in under **Access** can watch clips but not download them, and a streamer can turn
  off downloads of clips of their own stream. Off: nobody can download a clip from VillaNET. Every
  download is logged with who took which clip."

`configuration.go` gains the field beside the other Clips settings, `defaults.go` gains
`"AllowClipDownloads": false` (held to the manifest by `TestTheSectionDefaultsMatchTheManifest`), and
the settings backup carries it with no change of its own: it is built from the configuration struct.
`settingsNotIncluded`'s Clips line also names "which streams' streamers turned downloads off".

The **Clips on the server** card in the System Console keeps no download button (its help text says so
already). Downloading is a panel action for linked people.

### 1.2 The rule, on the server

One pure function decides it, used by the clip views and by the ticket route alike:

```go
// clipDownloadRefusal is why somebody may not download a clip, or "" when
// they may: the admin's switch, then the reader's access, then the clip's
// streamer. Whether the clip is still kept is the caller's (it is not a view
// at all otherwise).
func clipDownloadRefusal(on bool, linked bool, entry *clipEntry, doc *clipsDoc) clipErrorCode
```

- admin switch off: `downloads_off` (403), "Downloads are off.";
- not linked: `watch_only` / `locked` (403), from the gate, as every other clip route;
- the clip is gone: `clip_gone` (410), and no storage: `storage_unavailable` (503);
- its streamer blocked downloads: `streamer_downloads_off` (403), "The streamer has turned off
  downloads of their clips.".

`clipView` gains `canDownload` (always present, false by default). It is false for a let-in reader, in
the System Console's view, and for every refusal above. The cards of shared clips do not carry it.

### 1.3 The streamer's switch

- **Stored:** `clipsDoc.NoDownloads map[streamTag]clipMark` (`json:"noDownloads,omitempty"`), beside
  `OptOut`, written by the same compare-and-set, with the same `clipMark{By, At}`. Keyed by the stream,
  not the person, exactly as clipping's switch is: unlinking leaves it, relinking another stream does
  not carry it, and whoever links the stream next finds it as it was left. A clip records its stream's
  tag when it is cut (`clipEntry.StreamTag`), so the block reaches every clip of the stream, those
  already made and every one to come; a clip whose stream nobody could name (an empty tag) is never
  blocked.
- **Route:** `PUT /api/v1/clips/streamer` takes `{"downloads": bool}` as well as today's
  `{"allow": bool}`, exactly one of the two (400 otherwise), on the same stream (the caller's linked
  stream, by its tag), the same meter (`writeLimiter`) and the same class (linked). It answers
  `{"downloads": bool}`. It can be set whatever the admin's switch says; the panel only shows it while
  downloads are allowed. Logged ("A streamer turned downloads of their clips off/on", user id only).
- **`/me`:** `clipsStreamer` gains `downloads` (true: allowed), **present only while the admin allows
  downloads**, so its absence is what hides the switch. Absent with the rest of `clipsStreamer` when
  the clips document cannot be read.
- **Panel:** the second switch in **Clips of your stream**, after the notice of the first, while
  `me.clipsStreamer.downloads` is present. Label "Let people download clips of my stream". Its
  notice: "People who have linked their stream can download clips of yours." / "Nobody can download
  clips of your stream, those already made or any to come." After a change: "On. Download is back on
  every clip of your stream." / "Off. Download is gone from every clip of your stream." Its tooltip:
  "Whether people can download clips of your stream from VillaNET. It goes with the stream, like
  Allow clips of my stream." Turning it off wipes nothing and deletes nothing.
- **With Clips off** (3.2.1 review, finding sec-3): clips already made can still be watched and
  downloaded while **Let people clip streams** is off, or while Clips has nowhere to keep anything
  (the snapshot's `clips.enabled` is false), so the scissors and **Clips of your stream** stay for a
  linked streamer while `me.clipsStreamer.downloads` is present, with this switch alone in the sheet
  (`streamerView`'s `{kind: 'downloads'}`); clipping's switch, its notice and Wipe are not shown, and
  the scissors' dot says off. The server needed no change: the route and `/me` never depended on
  clipping being on.

### 1.4 The ticket and the download address

| Route | Class | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `POST /api/v1/clips/download-ticket` `{"id":"<clip id>"}` | linked (clip family: not in `clipWatchRoutes`) | Re-checks every rule of §1.2, then the meter, then makes a ticket: 32 random bytes, hex. Stored as `clip_ticket_<userId>` (its SHA-256, the clip id, when) with a 60 s expiry, one per person (a new one replaces the old). Answers `{"url": "/plugins/ceo.villa.villanet/api/v1/clips/download?t=<ticket>"}`. The CSRF check applies, as on every POST. |
| `GET /api/v1/clips/download?t=<ticket>` | linked | Reads the caller's ticket, compares it in constant time, checks its own clock (60 s), re-checks §1.2, then spends it (`KVCompareAndDelete`), logs the download, and answers **303** to `download?d=<address>`. Anything stale, spent, another person's or another clip's is **410**, a plain page, and writes nothing. |
| `GET /api/v1/clips/download?d=<address>` | linked | The download address: the clip id, when it lapses (ten minutes after the ticket was spent) and an HMAC of both and the person's id under the plugin's own secret (`handle_secret`), so it needs no storage and works on any node. Re-checks §1.2 on every request, then serves the file (§1.5). A wrong, lapsed or other person's address is 410, a plain page. |

- **Meter:** `downloadLimiter`, 10 tickets per person per 10 minutes, spent only once the request is
  known to be good (a refusal costs nothing), refunded when the store fails.
- **The clips' version** (the snapshot's `clips.version`, which an open viewer follows) also covers,
  while downloads are on, that they are on and which clips their streamer blocked, so a viewer's
  Download follows either switch at once. With downloads off it is 3.2.0's, byte for byte. The
  streamer's switch republishes the clips.
- **Order:** everything that refuses comes before anything is written. A refused request writes no
  state; the ticket is spent only by a request that is going to serve.
- **Both routes are in the clip family's own table** (`clipRoutes`), behind `requireClipsAccess`, and
  are linked because they are not in `clipWatchRoutes`. That table, not `routeClasses`, is where clip
  routes are classified: `ServeHTTP` hands `/api/v1/clips…` to `serveClips` before the switch, and
  `TestEveryRouteHasAnAccessClass` fails for any path in `routeClasses` that the switch does not serve.
  `TestClipGateClasses` holds every clip route to its table and tries each one as every kind of caller;
  both routes are added to its calls.
- **Evidence.** Mattermost 11.11.1 (`3acb3a7f684d`, `server/channels/app/plugin_requests.go:170-299`)
  sets `Mattermost-User-Id` on a GET carried by the session cookie without a CSRF token, and checks one
  on every other method, so the POST cannot be forged from another page and the GET carries the
  session. Mattermost Desktop v6.3.0 `src/main/downloadsManager.ts:98-136` cancels a new download
  (`event.preventDefault()`) and starts it again with `webContents.downloadURL(item.getURL())` after
  its save dialog; Electron's `getURL()` is Chromium's `DownloadItemImpl::GetURL()`, the **last** URL
  of the redirect chain (`components/download/internal/common/download_item_impl.cc:937`), which is
  the download address, not the spent ticket.

### 1.5 The file

- `Content-Type: video/mp4`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Cache-Control: no-store`,
  `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'; sandbox`, and Range, If-Range and 206 by
  `http.ServeContent`, through the same buffered writer the player's route uses. Nothing is read into
  memory: the file is streamed from the disk.
- **Name:** `<Streamer> - <title or "clip"> - <YYYY-MM-DD HHMM>.mp4`. The streamer is the clip's
  stream as the viewer shows it (the person's display name, or the stream's name); the time is when the
  clip starts (`startAt`, else when it was published), in the reader's Mattermost time zone, else UTC.
  Sanitised: control characters and `/ \ : * ? " < > |` become spaces, runs of spaces become one, the
  ends lose spaces and dots, the streamer is held to 40 characters and the title to 80; an empty part
  becomes "VillaNET" or "clip". `Content-Disposition: attachment; filename="<ASCII>"; filename*=UTF-8''<percent-encoded>`.
- **Log:** `LogInfo("A clip was downloaded", "user_id", …, "clip_id", …)` once per ticket spent. Never
  the ticket, the address or the file's path.
- **Stale page:** text/plain, 410, "This download link has run out or was already used. Press Download
  in VillaNET again."

### 1.6 The panel

- **Clip viewer:** a **Download** pill after Share and before Report and Delete, only while
  `clip.canDownload` and the reader is linked (`clipDownloadOffered`, pure, in `clipLogic.ts`, checked by
  `build/check-away.mjs`). When it goes by itself with focus on it (either switch moved while the
  viewer was open), focus goes to the pill that took its place, or to Back once the row is empty or
  gone (`useFocusHome` on the row and `actionAfterLoss`; 3.2.1 review finding web-1). Pressing it
  asks for a ticket ("Preparing…"), then starts the download through a temporary `<a href download>` on the answered address, which must begin with the plugin's own
  `/api/v1/clips/download?t=`; anything else is refused in the browser. Then "Your download has started."
  Refusals in plain words: "Downloads are off.", "The streamer has turned off downloads of their
  clips.", "This clip has gone.", "Slow down a little.", "The download could not be started just now."
- **The native player keeps `controlsList="nodownload"`**: the pill is the one way.

### 1.7 The lab

`webapp/lab/mocks/client.ts` and `world.ts` get the ticket, the address check and the streamer switch,
and two lab chips on the Clips rows: **downloads on** (the admin's switch) and **Victor blocks
downloads** (the streamer's block on the lab's own stream). The mock's download only counts itself.

## 2. No biggest-fan opt-out

Removed:

- `POST /api/v1/awards/fan`, its case in `ServeHTTP` and its access class; `POST
  /api/v1/admin/fans/restart`.
- Every use of `fan_optouts`: the copy in `awardDocs`, its reading and decoding, `fanOptoutState`, the
  per-pass purge (`purgeFansSoon`, `finishFanPurge`, the `purge` list), and "fans stop while the list
  cannot be read".
- `ledgerAlert.fansOff`, the airtime status's `fansOff`, the admin line "Biggest fans are off…" and the
  Airtime card's **Start biggest fans again**.
- `GET /awards/mine`'s `fanNamed` and `fansReadable`; `GET /takebacks`'s `fan` (it keeps `sounds`, for
  **Delete my sounds**).
- The You tab's **Biggest fan** switch; the own popover's "Don't name me as anybody's biggest fan",
  "You're not counted for biggest fans." and "Let VillaNET count me again".
- Their lab chips, types, words and checks, and the trophy button's "fans on" reason to show (it was
  there so the opt-out could always be reached).

Kept: `fans_totals` and its rule for bytes that will not decode (deleted and started from zero, never a
copy); the snapshot's `fansOn`, which now means **Name each streamer's biggest fan** and **Rank the Top
Snoopers** are both on; counting only while **Show who is watching** is on (`fansCounted`); the purge
machinery of Twitch, which is unrelated. The fan purge served only opt-outs, so it goes.

**At activation, once:** if `fan_optouts` is in the store, any viewer still on its `purge` list (a
deletion promised and not yet finished) has their rows taken out of `fans_totals` first, then the
document is deleted, with one Info line ("people", the number of entries it held; no ids). A
`quarantine_fan_optouts` copy is deleted with it, for the same reason: it names who opted out, and
nothing reads it. If the purge cannot be saved, the document stays for the next activation and a
warning says so. Nothing else is restored: what was deleted for people who opted out stays deleted, and
they are counted again from now, while faces are on.
